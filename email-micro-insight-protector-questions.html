Following up on our note yesterday regarding client shutdown during trauma work:

When you identify that a Protector part (intellectualizing, numbing, changing the subject, or sudden anger) has stepped in, here are 3 direct clinical questions from the IFS & structural dissociation frameworks that help unblend the client without triggering a defensive spiral:

1. "How long have you been carrying this job to protect them?"

Why it works: Validates the protector's exhaustion. Most protectors have been working since childhood and feel deeply relieved when their labor is acknowledged.

2. "What are you worried might happen if you stepped back just 5% right now?"

Why it works: Reveals the core catastrophic fear guarding the wounded exile without prematurely exposing the client to raw emotional flooding.

3. "How old does this protective part think you are today?"

Why it works: Disrupts traumatic time-distortion. Protectors often believe the client is still 7 or 12 years old and unable to handle adult reality.
