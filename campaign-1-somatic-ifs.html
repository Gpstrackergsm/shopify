A quick clinical insight on working with somatic dysregulation:

When clients get stuck in fight/flight hyperarousal or freeze hypoarousal, top-down cognitive restructuring ("let's examine that thought") often fails because the prefrontal cortex is partially offline.

Using bottom-up somatic tracking allows the client's nervous system to re-enter their Window of Tolerance before processing relational or developmental trauma:

"Notice where that tension is living in your chest or throat right now. If that sensation had a shape or temperature, what would it be?"
